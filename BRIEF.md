# Learn Japanese Grammar

## What it is (one sentence)
A site that helps a person who knows the very basics of Japanese learn beginner grammar until they are able to understand daily conversations.

## Who it's for
Myself, and anyone who wants to learn Japanese but don't know where to start.

## The 3 things it MUST do
1. Teach legitimate beginner Japanese Grammar
2. Have Lessons and Exercizes with a grader to practice what you learned
3. The website is easy to use and navigate
